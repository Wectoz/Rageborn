Macros
Create character macros for spells you know.
RB Charge
Charge out of combat, Intercept in Berserker Stance; otherwise switches stance.
Create
#showtooltip /cast [stance:1,nocombat] Charge; [stance:3] Intercept; [combat] Berserker Stance; Battle Stance
RB Overpower
Overpower from any stance: switches to Battle Stance first.
Exists
#showtooltip Overpower /cast [nostance:1] Battle Stance; Overpower
You already have a macro with this name.
RB Pummel
Pummel from any stance: switches to Berserker Stance first.
Create
#showtooltip Pummel /cast [nostance:3] Berserker Stance; Pummel
RB Shield Bash
Equips your shield, then Shield Bash; from Berserker Stance it switches first.
Create
#showtooltip Shield Bash /equipslot [noworn:Shields] 17 <shield> /cast [stance:1/2] Shield Bash; Defensive Stance
