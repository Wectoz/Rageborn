RB Charge
Charge out of combat, Intercept in Berserker Stance; otherwise switches stance.
Create
#showtooltip /dismount /startattack /cast [stance:1/2,nocombat] Charge; [stance:3] Intercept; [combat] Berserker Stance; Battle Stance
RB Overpower
Overpower from any stance: switches to Battle Stance first.
Create
#showtooltip Overpower /startattack /cast [nostance:1] Battle Stance; Overpower
RB Pummel
Pummel from any stance: switches to Berserker Stance first.
Create
#showtooltip Pummel /startattack /cast [nostance:3] Berserker Stance; Pummel
RB Rage Shout
Bloodrage, then Battle Shout, on one button.
Create
#showtooltip Battle Shout /cast Bloodrage /cast Battle Shout
